How This Works
There's no separate point-of-sale history in this project, so a detection at checkout is treated as the closest thing to a sale. For each product, scans from the last 14 days are bucketed by day and fit with a simple linear trend, then projected forward 7 days. The suggested reorder quantity adds a 7-day lead time and a 25% safety buffer on top of that projection. Predicted revenue multiplies predicted demand by the price set in the Product Catalog.
